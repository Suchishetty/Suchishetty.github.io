Featured Project

AutoHeal: AI-Powered Self-Healing Application Platform

A monitoring platform that checks application health over HTTP, detects consecutive failures, and runs rule-based recovery actions. After each recovery it verifies that the service is healthy again. Gemini AI then analyzes the incident and writes a plain-language root-cause diagnosis with recommendations.

React Node.js Express PostgreSQL Gemini AI

Highlights

  • Failure detection based on consecutive health-check results
  • Automated recovery with post-recovery verification
  • AI-generated incident diagnoses and recommendations

VTU Genius: AI-Powered VTU Exam Assistant

A Django app where VTU students upload PDF notes, ask questions, and prepare for exams. A Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline built on ChromaDB and Sentence Transformers grounds each answer in the student's own notes. Deployed on Render with PostgreSQL and Gunicorn.
